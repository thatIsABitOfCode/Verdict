# Verdict
Verdict - Justice. Simplified.

## Project Overview

**Verdict** is an AI-powered justice platform designed to help people understand their legal rights, prepare their legal matters, and connect with the appropriate legal support.

The platform is being developed as part of a **technology competition/hackathon** with the goal of exploring how AI and technology can improve access to justice.

---

## The Problem

For many people, accessing the legal system can be difficult, expensive, and confusing.

People often don't know:

- Whether they have a legitimate legal issue.
- What their rights are.
- What evidence they need.
- Where to get legal assistance.
- Which organisation or professional they should approach.
- What steps they should take first.

Although legal assistance exists, many people struggle to navigate the system and understand where to begin.

---

## Proposed Solution

**Verdict** aims to bridge the gap between people and the legal system by providing an accessible platform that helps users take the right first steps.

The platform will allow users to:

- Describe their legal situation in simple language.
- Understand the general nature of their legal issue.
- Learn about potentially relevant rights and information.
- Identify and organise important evidence.
- Build a timeline of events.
- Prepare information before consulting a legal professional.
- Find relevant legal support and resources.

## Planned Technology

The exact technology stack is currently being evaluated and may evolve during development.

Planned technologies include:

-Frontend: Web-based application
-Backend: Python-based backend
-AI: Large Language Model (LLM) / AI API
-Database: SQL-based database
-Authentication: Secure user authentication
-APIs: REST API architecture
-Version Control: Git & GitHub
-Deployment: Cloud-based hosting

The technology choices may change as the project develops and the requirements of the competition become clearer.
